Managing the NELVA shop

Everything you change here (products, prices, photos, ingredients and categories) updates the website for you. No code needed.

The one rule: saving keeps your changes, but they only appear on the website when you click Publish to website (bottom left) and then OK. Make all your changes first, then publish once.

1. Log in

  1. Go to nelvanatural.netlify.app/admin and bookmark it.
  2. Click Login with GitHub, sign in, then click Authorize.
  3. The first time on a new phone or computer, click Publish to website once and paste the Publish link your developer gave you.

2. Add a new product

  1. Products → + Product.
  2. Fill in the full name, choose the category, set Status to Available to buy and type the price (numbers only, e.g. 20000).
  3. Add the size, a photo (Choose an image → Upload) and the short text for the product card.
  4. Fill in the Description, Ingredients and How to use from the label.
  5. Click Publish → Publish now at the top to save, then Publish to website when you’re done.

Not ready to sell yet? Set Status to Coming soon: it shows on the homepage with “Coming Soon” and can’t be ordered.

3. Change a product

  • Products → click the product → change what you need → Publish → Publish now.
  • Find products faster: use Filter by at the top of the list to show one category, or Coming soon / Sold out / Hidden.
  • Avoid changing the full name of a product people have already ordered. Change the Short name instead.

4. Labels, sold out and hiding

  • Label on the photo: Bestseller, New or Sale.
  • Sold out: tick it to stop orders but keep the product visible. Untick when it’s back.
  • Hide from the shop: takes it off the website without deleting it.
  • Position in the shop: lower numbers appear first.
  • Search words: words customers might type, e.g. dandruff dry curls frizz.

5. Categories

  • Shop settings → Categories: these are the “Shop By Need” tiles on the homepage and the Shop links in the footer.
  • To rename one, change only its Name. Never change the short code of an existing category.
  • Deleting a category that still has products shows a warning first. Click Cancel to keep it.

6. Photos and publishing

  • Portrait photos with the product in the centre look best. Big phone photos are fine; the website shrinks them.
  • Publish once after all your edits. Your changes are live in about 1–2 minutes.

Something not looking right? Don’t publish again; contact your web developer.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what information NELVA NATURAL ("we", "us") collects when you use this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the site you agree to this policy.

What we collect
  • Order details you enter at checkout: your name, phone number, email address, delivery address and any order notes.
  • Newsletter sign-ups: the email address you submit to join our mailing list.
  • Messages you send us through the contact form.
  • Basic technical data your browser sends automatically, such as your device type and general location, used only to keep the site working.
How we use it
  • To process and deliver your order, and to contact you about it.
  • To respond to your enquiries.
  • To send you newsletters and offers, only if you have subscribed. Every newsletter has an unsubscribe link.
Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the services we use to run the shop:

  • Paystack to process card payments.
  • Delivery partners to fulfil your order.
  • Brevo to manage our newsletter list.
  • Formspree and EmailJS to deliver order and contact emails.

Each of these providers handles your data under its own privacy policy.

Your cart

Your shopping cart is stored only in your own browser (local storage). It is not sent to us until you place an order, and clearing your browser data removes it.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you what personal data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email nelvanaturals@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Help & FAQs

Shipping & Returns

Order processing

Orders are prepared within 1 to 2 business days. We confirm your order and the delivery fee with you on WhatsApp before dispatch.

Where we deliver

  • Lagos: 1 to 3 business days after dispatch.
  • Rest of Nigeria: 3 to 7 business days after dispatch, by courier.

Delivery fees

The fee depends on your location and is confirmed on your order. Payment is by card at checkout or by transfer arranged on WhatsApp.

Tracking

Once your order is dispatched we send you the courier details and any tracking number on WhatsApp. See Track your order below.

Returns

Because our products are haircare items, we can only accept returns of unopened, unused products in their original packaging, within 7 days of delivery.

If your order arrives damaged, or you received the wrong item, contact us within 48 hours of delivery at nelvanaturals@gmail.com with your order reference and a photo. We will replace it or refund you.

Approved refunds are made to your original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. Delivery fees are non-refundable unless the return is our error.

Not returnable

  • Opened or used products, for hygiene reasons.
  • Items returned after 7 days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are your products really natural?

Yes. Our formulas are made with botanical extracts and cold-pressed oils, and are free from sulfates and parabens. They are cruelty-free and vegan.

Should I do a patch test?

Yes. Apply a small amount to the inside of your forearm and wait 24 hours before first use. Stop using the product if you notice irritation.

How do I place an order?

Add items to your cart and go to checkout. You can either complete the order on WhatsApp with your details pre-filled, or pay by card through Paystack.

What payment methods do you accept?

Card payments through Paystack at checkout, or bank transfer arranged with us on WhatsApp.

Do you deliver outside Lagos?

Yes, we deliver nationwide by courier. See Shipping & Returns for timeframes.

How long will delivery take?

1 to 3 business days within Lagos and 3 to 7 business days elsewhere in Nigeria, after dispatch.

Can I return a product?

Unopened products can be returned within 7 days. Opened haircare products cannot be returned for hygiene reasons. Full details are in Shipping & Returns.

Do you offer wholesale or bulk orders?

Yes. Email nelvanaturals@gmail.com with what you need and we will get back to you.

Track Your Order

We do not have an automated order tracker yet. To check on your order, message us on WhatsApp with your order reference (it looks like NN-XXXXXX and is shown on your confirmation screen and email).

We will reply with your order status and courier details.
